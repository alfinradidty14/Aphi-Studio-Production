💡 2-Minute Quick Setup:

  1. Visit supabase.com and create a free account.
  2. Click New Project, then navigate to Project Settings → API.
  3. Copy your Project URL and anon / public key into the fields on the left.
  4. Click Save Configuration, and your entire project will sync securely to the cloud.
